About

Jué Chen · 陳覺

How silence looks, and breaks.

I photograph what is held back. A face behind a bubble, a mirror, the shadow of leaves. A room with only the legs of the person in it. A coast seen through the window of whatever is carrying me past it.

I am interested in the moment before something is said, and in the small event that ends it: a hand entering the light, a swimmer breaking the surface.

Photographs, 2012 to now.
